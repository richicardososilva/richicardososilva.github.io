Molecular & cell biologist

Hello, I’m Richard.

I study how cells protect and repair their DNA, and how the way DNA is packaged influences what happens when it gets damaged.

My work brings together chromatin biology, molecular tool development, live-cell imaging and proteomics. I am especially interested in the proteins that respond to DNA damage in different parts of the genome.

Originally from São Paulo, I have worked in Brazil, New Zealand, Germany, Switzerland and I'm currently based in the Netherlands. Outside the lab, I enjoy bouldering, hiking and reading.

A few things I work on

01

DNA repair in its chromatin context

Why is some DNA harder to repair than other DNA? I study how the local organisation of chromosomes shapes the cellular response to breaks.

02

Seeing DNA damage as it happens

I develop molecular sensors that let us follow damaged chromatin in living cells and identify proteins nearby.

03

Proteins around damaged DNA

I use proximity proteomics to explore which proteins gather near DNA damage, and how these protein networks change with cellular conditions.
